**Title:** Location Theories vs Reality in Dhaka Division

**Summary:** This project tested classic location theories against upazila population patterns in Dhaka Division using hotspot analysis, Local Moran's I clustering and pattern mapping. Narayanganj Sadar, Savar and Gazipur Sadar emerged as 99%-confidence hotspots around textile hubs, with no regular geometric hierarchy around them. The pattern fits growth pole theory rather than central place theory.
